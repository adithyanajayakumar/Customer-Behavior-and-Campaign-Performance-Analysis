 Customer Behavior & Campaign Performance Analysis

# Overview

This project analyzes customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and marketing campaign performance using Excel and Power BI. The objective is to identify high-value customer segments, evaluate campaign effectiveness, and generate actionable business insights to support marketing decisions.

# Business Objectives

* Identify high-value customer segments
* Analyze marketing campaign effectiveness
* Understand customer purchasing behavior
* Evaluate product performance
* Provide recommendations to improve marketing outcomes

# Key Insights

* Premium-income customers exhibit the highest spending and campaign response rates.
* VIP customers are significantly more responsive to marketing campaigns than low-value customers.
* Recently active customers respond more frequently than at-risk customers.
* The latest marketing campaign achieved the highest acceptance rate.
* Wine products generate the largest share of total revenue.
